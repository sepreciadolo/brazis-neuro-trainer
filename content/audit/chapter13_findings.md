## Possible clinical errors (original left unchanged; decide in the review tool)

- **ch13-004**: Book p. 421 says a right hemispheric lesion weakens turning the head AWAY from the lesion, i.e., to the left (weak right SCM). The vignette describes a right hemispheric stroke with difficulty turning the head to the RIGHT and strong turning to the left, which is the opposite of the book and of the question's own explanation. Also the book says the SCM pathway is unsettled (truly ipsilateral, via the corpus callosum, or a double decussation) and calls the input 'chiefly' ipsilateral, so 'uncrossed corticobulbar' is oversimplified. Replacement question written.
